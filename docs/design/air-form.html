How to read it

Spikes
Pollution. PM2.5 is a fur of thin needles, PM10 a few thorns, ozone and NO₂ their own clusters. Longer, sharper and more spread out as the reading rises. A reading of 0 has no spike at all.
Beads, tufts, burrs
Pollen. Tree is round beads, grass is soft tufts, weed is a burr with tiny prickles (ragweed). More of them as the level rises.
The body
Weather. Round when it's mild. Swollen and slumped when it's hot and muggy. Hard and terraced when it's cold. Gritty when it's dry, glossy when it's humid.
Toothed bands
Change since yesterday. Temperature at the waist, humidity above it, pressure below. Deeper, faster teeth for a bigger swing. Steady weather has no band.
Dots
Yesterday's body, so you can see it grow, shrink or slump.
