The rule

  1. A flower stands when it is one of yours, or when it is well up right now.
  2. Everything else is a bud on the ground: there, quiet, and you can still tap it.
  3. Each flower keeps its place from day to day. Only its height and how far it opens change.
  4. A thread joins two flowers when together they make a kind of day.

Tried and dropped

A different shape per family, and a lean for below or above normal. Both made the garden harder to read (owner, Nov 19), so direction and detail moved into words.

Open questions

  1. How many can stand? The study lets up to seven stand. Fewer?
  2. Does a bud grow back down? When a factor stops being yours, does its flower shrink over weeks, or stay?
  3. Body data. Under the fold until it turns out to matter, then a sprout. Which Health readings are worth the permission?
