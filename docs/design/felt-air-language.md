# felt air: a shared language

Names for the pieces of the app, so we can talk about them (and so code, docs and crits use the same words). Proposed by Claude, Oct 16, 2026, for Pandji and April to edit.

## Things you log

| Word | Means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Moment** | One saved time: when, where, and the air around it. | "log", "entry", "event" |
| **Rescue** | A moment with the rescue inhaler. Orange. | "attack" (never on screen) |
| **Standard** | A moment with the daily (maintenance) inhaler. Blue. | "maintenance" on screen |
| **Baseline** | The ordinary days felt air compares against: moments it saves on its own, without an inhaler, and your first capture. | "calm", "I'm okay", "usual days" on screen (decided Oct 16, 2026) |
| **Note** | Something you said with no inhaler logged (Voice on Now). Kept with the air; never in patterns. | "entry" |
| **Voice** / **Body** | Stops on the rail: your words and tags (slide into the panel to record, lift to stop, then Save), and your activity and Apple Health in words. A rescue with nothing said wears a hollow ring on the timeline and a dot on Voice. | "Self", "journal" on screen |
| **Flare day** | A day with rescue use. Patterns compare flare days with your baseline. | "attack", "bad day" |

## The picture of the air

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Sky** | A moment's conditions drawn as color. The main visual of the app. |
| **Base** | The sky's foundation: **hue** = temperature, **light** = time of day, **softness** = humidity. |
| **Layers** | Colors added on top of the base, one per thing in the air: PM2.5 slate, PM10 sand, NO₂ rust, ozone icy cyan, tree green, grass gold, weed violet. Faint when low, fuller when high. |
| **Swatch** | A moment at small size: a dot of its sky with an orange or blue **ring** for its type. Used in the timeline, Days and the calendar. |
| **Reading** | A single measured value with its unit and source ("9 ppb, OpenAQ"). |
| **Level** | Where a reading sits on its own official scale: **Good / OK / Not good** (air and pollen only). |
| **Amount bars** | Three bars showing a level as an amount: one bar a little, three a lot. |
| **Weather words** | Temperature and humidity in plain words (Mild, Hot, Humid). Never judged good or bad. |

## Moving around

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Zoom** | Moving between the four **scales**: **Month · Days · Moment · Details**. |
| **Gauge** | The four dots on the right that show the scale you're on. Hold and slide it to zoom. |
| **Anchor** | The one thing carried through every scale (the current moment's sky → its day tile → its month square → its map), so the zoom reads as one continuous move. |
| **Timeline** | The track at the bottom of the Moment scale: ticks for hours and days, swatches for moments. |
| **Playhead** | The white line in the middle of the timeline, with the **time pill** above it. Whatever is under it is the current moment. |
| **Scrub** | Dragging the timeline to move through moments. |
| **Hold to read** | Touching and holding the sky to name the layer under your finger, with the **pulse** (a haptic that quickens as the level nears high). |
| **Pull to log** | Pulling up on the **+**: a short pull is Rescue, all the way up is Standard, letting go early cancels. |
| **Ring button** | The Insights button beside the +. Its ring fills while felt air is still **learning**; a **bubble** appears when there's a **pattern**. |

## Product words

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Pattern** | Conditions that show up more on flare days than in your baseline. Only shown once there's enough data. |
| **Look-ahead** | Days coming up that look like your flare days. Never a prediction or a risk percentage. |
| **felt button** | The physical button: orange cap Rescue, teal cap Standard. |
| **Design crit switches** | Settings › Design crit: live options for comparing designs on the phone. |

## Rules of the language

- Plain and short. Say what happens.
- Never "attack", never a percent risk, never cause ("the ozone caused…").
- Outdoor air only: say "outside" or "near you", never "the air you breathed".
- "Color", American spelling.
