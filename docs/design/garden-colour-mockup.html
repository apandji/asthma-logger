Study 3

Insights keeps its neutral ink

The page stays neutral. The link back to the garden is the small flower beside each row, in its garden colour, and bigger when it is one of yours, the same as in the garden. Exercise and Colds and flu have no flower: they are about you, not the air.

Tap a row for a second layer: the typical reading on inhaler days beside ordinary days, with units. Words first, readings if you ask. The values here are examples.

Open questions

  1. Normal is decided. Normal for this place and this week (owner, Nov 19). It comes from the app's own recent hours at this place and time of day, not from long-term averages.
  2. Red beside orange. Irritants in red sit near the orange of your inhaler moments on the timeline. Far enough apart?
  3. Eight flowers. Splitting pollen into three makes eight. Too many for the phone?
  4. How deep do the layers go? Words, then typical readings. A third layer with counts ("8 of 14 inhaler days") was ruled out on Oct 17. Reopen it?
