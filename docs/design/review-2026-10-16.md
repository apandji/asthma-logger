# Design review, Oct 16 2026 (before the morning crit)

Two passes on the build installed on the phone overnight: Claude's own review (with full context) and an independent critic (a fresh agent that saw only screenshots and a one-line description of the app). Screens reviewed: home, moment details, onboarding map, Data view, calendar.

## What changed tonight

- No tab bar. The Journal is one surface: swipe between moments, + to log, ring button for Insights, pinch in for the calendar, turn sideways for Data, "felt air" menu for All moments and Settings.
- The header and the colour field follow the moment you're on. New moments slide in from the +, the felt button or the watch.
- The colour field reads as weather. Hue is temperature, grey haze is dirtier air, mist is humidity, specks are pollen, with a "Reading the colour" legend on the details page.
- Demo pollen (realistic Midwest seasons, labelled Demo).
- Design crit switches for every open question (Settings › Design crit).

## Independent critic: top points, and what we did

| Point | Status |
|---|---|
| Floating + and Insights covered the details page | **Fixed.** They live on the home only. |
| Pinch is hard to discover and hard for tremor / one hand / VoiceOver | **Fixed in part.** Tapping a moment opens it, the "felt air" menu has All moments, pinch stays as a shortcut. VoiceOver actions on the + and the moments. |
| "Maintenance" on the card vs "Standard" elsewhere | **Fixed.** "Standard" everywhere. |
| The learning text was cut off ("6 of 20 calm mom…") | **Replaced** by the ring button (ring fills while learning). The strip is still a crit option. |
| Empty values (Dew point "—", big "No location" box) | **Fixed.** Empty weather tiles are hidden and a missing map is one line on the card style. |
| "Reading the colour" was the first thing on the details page | **Fixed.** It starts collapsed. |
| Chart axis "M" for midnight | **Fixed.** Shows "12 AM". |
| Temperature and humidity "Good / Not good" is a health judgement the app can't back | **Open, worth discussing.** Proposal: keep tiers for air and pollen, and use plain words for weather (Mild, Hot, Humid). |
| Pollen specks look like dust on the screen | **Open.** Tune the size and opacity, and keep them out from behind text. |
| Faint grey secondary text may fail contrast on glass | **Open.** Needs a contrast pass on device in light and dark. |

## Claude's review (interaction and visual)

**Working**
- One moment at a time, with the room taking its colour, is the clearest version of the "ambient" idea so far. The header, the field and the moment all say the same thing.
- The no-card moment (a map in a soft circle floating on the field) is calmer than any card version.
- The + has a physical story: a little liquid at rest, rising with your finger, a short pull for Rescue.
- The ring button gives Insights a reason to exist before there's a pattern.

**Problems, worst first**
1. **Demo moments have no location**, so on a fresh demo the no-card circles are empty. Demo with real moments, or give the demo week coordinates near home.
2. **Duplication:** the header says "Standard · Yesterday, 7:55 AM" and the moment repeats "Standard · 7:55 AM". Keep one; the one under the circle could become just the voice note.
3. **The instruction line** ("Tap or pinch open for details · pinch in for the calendar") is chrome on a calm screen. Show it on the first few launches only.
4. **Pinch-in competes with the horizontal swipe.** It didn't trigger reliably in the simulator; the menu path works. Check on device; if it's flaky, use a two-finger pinch on the header area or keep the menu as the main path.
5. **The cards-to-dots morph only animates the moments on screen.** The other dots fade in. It reads well for the current moment; judge it on device.
6. **The speech bubble's position is tuned for one phone width.** Check on the 15 Pro.
7. **The sideways Data view wasn't verified in the simulator** (no rotation). Check on device.

## What to try in the crit (Settings › Design crit)

- Moment style: No card ↔ Card. Card label: Quiet tab / Dot / Glow / Bold tab.
- Header: The moment ↔ Right now.
- Colour field: Weather ↔ Original inks. Pollen (demo): This season / Spring trees / Late-summer ragweed.
- Insights: Ring button / Peek strip / Edge tab. Insights state: Demo learning / Demo learned (bubble).
- + pull order: Rescue first ↔ Standard first.
- Good / OK / Not good: Signal bars / Word / Shape / Gauge / Track.
