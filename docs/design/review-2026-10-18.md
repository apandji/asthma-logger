# Overnight review, Oct 18, 2026

What was built while you slept, what I checked, and what to look at first. Everything is on one branch,
`claude/the-log`, stacked on #75 (top controls): [apandji/asthma-logger#76](https://github.com/apandji/asthma-logger/pull/76). The pass it follows is [polish-pass.md](polish-pass.md); new

## Look at these first, on the phone

1. **Pull the +.** Press, pull up into Rescue, lift. Then once more within two minutes (it adds a puff). Then
   tap the + instead of pulling (the well stays open; tap Rescue or outside). Does the glass well feel like
   part of the system? Are the haptics right (soft into Rescue, firmer into Standard, success on the log)?
2. **Drag the timeline slowly** across a few moments and into the forecast. The colour should flow, the
   words should snap. Any flicker when you land?
3. **Hold the rail on Voice, slide in, say a sentence, lift.** The words should appear as you speak. The
   stop you pressed should stay under your thumb when the rail opens. Then say Save.
4. **Tap the sparkle.** Insights should grow out of the button and start in the home's colour. (The sheet
   didn't zoom in the simulator every time; I'd like to know what the phone does.)
5. **Hold the rail anywhere** and slide: the stop under your thumb grows, neighbours ease away, the whole
   thing sits in one glass well.
6. **Listen.** A tick on a log, a breath when a data set lands on "learned". Ringer on. Settings › Logging ›
   Sounds turns them off. Keep or kill?

## What changed, by commit

### 1. The log, pulled (`62f282b`)
- `PullToLog`: the + grows into a glass well (`GlassEffectContainer` morph), Rescue nearest the thumb,
  Standard above, a lens follows the finger. Lift in a zone logs; lift low cancels; tap latches it open for a
  second tap, and a touch anywhere else (or 4 s) closes it. VoiceOver and Design crit › Log get the menu.
- The new tick drops onto the timeline. The ink ripple starts from the +'s side (it was fixed to the right).
- No toast. The header already says "Rescue logged"; Undo sits on the capture card for 10 s.
- Lock Screen standby card: "Here if you need it. / The mic logs a rescue and listens." and a grey, barely
  visible ring until a moment arrives. (You flagged the old copy; the provider sheet still lists it.)

### 2. Finish (`ffddd15`)
- **Continuous sky while scrubbing.** One `NowSky` for every time, so its identity holds while you drag and
  the mesh eases between readings; the temperature hue is interpolated between the readings either side of
  the playhead. Landed, the sky takes the moment's id again, so arriving somewhere new still crossfades.
- The home sentence blurs between readings (`blurReplace`), matching `ScaleHeader`.
- The verdict dot breathes only on "Matches your flare days". The sparkle button nods when a sentence with a
  sparkle lands. The sparkle key's title is set in the sentence face.
- A temperature drop is said once: "The temperature had fallen 19° in three hours. ✦", not followed by
  "A sharp temperature drop was there."
- Tips' buttons and Settings' switches in the app's ink (primary / standard blue), not system blue / green.
- The Now pill keeps one width. Reduce Motion now also stills the probe overlay's springs.

### 3. Live words, the rail well, the Insights hero, sounds (`1440f10`)
- **`VoiceNotes` records through `AVAudioEngine`.** The tap writes the AAC file and feeds on-device
  `SFSpeechAudioBufferRecognitionRequest` with partial results; `liveWords` updates as you speak. `LiveWords`
  shows them in the mini panel (newest word brighter, tail kept, head truncation). The file is only
  transcribed afterwards when live recognition is off. `finishLive()` waits up to 2 s for the final result.
- **The rail in a glass well**, and a lens: the stop under the thumb grows (×1.3), neighbours ease away.
- **Insights grows from the sparkle** (`navigationTransition(.zoom)`), starts in the home sky's colour and
  blooms into your factors over 1.2 s; the archetype name arrives 220 ms after the page.
- "Checking the air near you…" shimmers. The sentence steps to 22 pt at accessibility sizes.
- Two synthesised sounds (`Resources/Sounds/log.wav`, `learned.wav`), ambient category (the ringer rules),
  Settings › Logging › Sounds. `Sounds.play` skips while recording.

### 4. Fixes found while testing (last commit)
- **The rail grows around your thumb.** Opening the rail used to slide every stop to make room, so the
  selection jumped to whatever landed under the finger. `railShift` keeps the pressed stop where it was.
- **The Insights/Settings pill didn't take taps.** Interactive glass on a container swallows its buttons'
  touches. The container is plain glass now. (This was broken in #75 too.)
- The pill steps aside while the rail is open, pinned or held.
- The engine tap closure had to be `@Sendable` (it runs on the audio thread; the app's default is main actor).
