# Review: presentation pass, Nov 13, 2026

For the Friday, Nov 20 presentation. Branch `claude/presentation-cleanup`, on top of `main` (live Ambee, PR #79)
plus April's latest commit on `april/redesign`.

## What changed

**One look.** The app opens in April's chart-led look (Menus, combined chart): `Crit.AppLook.standard`. The sky
look and the Apple Health look are still there as Debug options.

**Settings.** "Demo and development" is gone from the top. Under About there is a Version row: five taps show a
**Scenarios** row (five more hide it). Scenarios holds the demo run, loadouts, data sets and the staged days, in
every build. Open design questions, the other looks, test tools and prototypes are in Scenarios › Design and
development, Debug builds only. The Rail picker only shows in the sky look.

**The chart no longer joins moments (owner: "we're capturing moments and not continuous").** Before, each line
was a smooth curve through the readings stamped on logged moments, which could be a day apart and at different
places. Now:
- A line is the air hour by hour at one place: Apple Weather's past hours (temperature, humidity, wind) and
  Ambee's hourly history (PM2.5, ozone, pollen), then the forecast.
- Hours more than about three apart aren't joined. A gap stays a gap.
- The forecast is dashed and paler: it's a guess.
- Moments are marks at their own readings: a baseline is a small hollow ring, a rescue an orange dot, a standard
  dose a blue one. This is the comparison the engine makes, on screen.
- Environment's subtitle reads "Outdoor, hour by hour. The dots are your moments."

**A second drawing to compare.** Scenarios › Design and development › Looks › Chart: "Filled waves (April)" is
the default; "Quiet lines, orange for yours" draws every line thin and grey with no fill, so the only colour is
the moments'. Same data either way. Not decided; for Pandji and April to compare on the phone.

**Real data.**
- Baselines now carry Ambee's particles, ozone and pollen for their hour (they had Apple Weather and OpenAQ
  only, and there is no OpenAQ key on the demo Mac, so they had no air at all). Without this the engine can't
  compare air between baselines and inhaler moments.
- Ambee's POC key only reaches **two days** back ("Only past 2 days data available!", a 400 for the whole
  request). The cache never asks beyond that. A baseline filled more than two days late gets no Ambee air.
- A cache bug found on the way: a failed history request was remembered as "Ambee has nothing for these hours".
  Fixed (both-or-nothing history, and only gaps inside an answer are remembered); the cache file is renamed so
  no phone keeps the bad entries.
