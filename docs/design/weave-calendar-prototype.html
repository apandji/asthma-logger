Try it. Scroll the week sideways. Pinch two fingers together on the strip (or ctrl-scroll on a trackpad, or tap Month) for the calendar; pinch apart or tap a day to come back. Tap any day. Switch factors on the chart.

Each swatch is one moment (a time you used your inhaler). Four layers overprint inside it. Each factor is put into a band, and the band sets the density of its weave: sparse dots (low), checker (medium), fine weave (high). Each factor also has its own angle, so the layers stay distinct without colour.

Demo data. Pollen values are made up: felt air has no pollen source yet, so in the app this layer stays empty and labelled. The chart line only joins captured readings; it isn't continuous measurement.
